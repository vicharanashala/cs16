===== QA SECTION =====

1.1 What is Samagama?
§ Samagama is a community-driven Q&A and FAQ platform designed to help students and professionals find answers to their questions quickly and efficiently.

1.2 How do I create an account?
§ Click the "Sign Up" button on the homepage, fill in your name, email, and password, and click "Create Account". You'll receive a verification email to confirm your account.

1.3 What are the system requirements?
§ Samagama works on any modern web browser including Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge. No special software installation is required.

2.1 How do I ask a question?
§ Log in to your account, click "Ask a Question", enter your question title and details, add relevant tags, and click "Submit". Your question will be visible to the community.

2.2 Can I edit my question after posting?
§ Yes, you can edit your question within 24 hours of posting by clicking the edit icon next to your question. After 24 hours, you can request a moderator to make changes.

2.3 What makes a good question?
§ A good question is clear, specific, and has enough context. Avoid asking the same question multiple times, search existing FAQs first, and be respectful to other community members.

3.1 How do I answer a question?
§ Click on any open question, scroll to the bottom, and use the answer box to type your response. Click "Submit Answer" and your answer will appear in the thread. You can earn reputation points for helpful answers.

3.2 Can I change my answer?
§ Yes, you can edit your answer by clicking the edit button. Once accepted as the best answer by the question author, you can still edit it but other users will be notified of the changes.

3.3 What is reputation?
§ Reputation points reflect your contributions to the community. You earn points by asking good questions, providing helpful answers, and helping others. High reputation unlocks special privileges like moderating content.
